# Powell 10 AM — Live Trade Checklist

*Print this. Run through it in sequence every trading morning.*

---

## PRE-SESSION (Before 9:30 AM)

- [ ] Check ForexFactory / Briefing.com for 10:00 AM news events
  - If red-folder news at 10:00 AM → **SMT required** (not optional)
  - If Fed Chair speaks at 10:00 AM → **SMT required**
- [ ] Mark **PDH** (Previous Day High) on chart
- [ ] Mark **PDL** (Previous Day Low) on chart
- [ ] Note if today is **Friday** → Skip if no SMT, reduce size 50% if trading
- [ ] Set stop-loss order ready to place immediately (don't enter without a stop plan)

---

## OPENING RANGE (9:30 – 9:55 AM ET)

Watch. Do not trade. Build context.

- [ ] Mark **OR High** (highest point of any bar 9:30–9:55)
- [ ] Mark **OR Low** (lowest point of any bar 9:30–9:55)
- [ ] Observe where price sits relative to VWAP (above = bullish lean)
- [ ] Observe where price sits relative to EMA 21 (above = bullish lean)

---

## BIAS CHECK — 9:55 AM BAR CLOSE

Score each condition. **Require ≥ 2/3 to trade.**

| Condition | Bull (+1) | Bear (+1) | Your Score |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| VWAP | Close > VWAP | Close < VWAP | ___ |
| OR Midpoint | Close > OR Mid | Close < OR Mid | ___ |
| EMA 21 | Close > EMA 21 | Close < EMA 21 | ___ |
| **Total** | | | ___ /3 |

- Score ≥ 2 Bull → Watch for **LONG** setup
- Score ≥ 2 Bear → Watch for **SHORT** setup
- Score ≤ 1 either way → **NO TRADE TODAY**

---

## 10 AM CANDLE (10:00 – 10:05 AM ET)

Do NOT enter during this candle. Watch.

### Long Setup Requirements (all must be true):
- [ ] Candle wick extends **≥ 5 NQ points BELOW** OR Low (or prior swing low)
- [ ] Candle **CLOSES ABOVE** the swept level
- [ ] ES chart check: Did ES sweep its equivalent low? If NO → **SMT Confirmed ✅**
- [ ] SMT present OR setup is strong enough without it (dealer's choice if no SMT required)

### Short Setup Requirements (all must be true):
- [ ] Candle wick extends **≥ 5 NQ points ABOVE** OR High (or prior swing high)
- [ ] Candle **CLOSES BELOW** the swept level
- [ ] ES chart check: Did ES sweep its equivalent high? If NO → **SMT Confirmed ✅**

---

## ENTRY

### V1.0 (Aggressive):
- [ ] At 10:05 AM bar open → Enter at market
- [ ] Stop: **3 points beyond the sweep wick extreme**
- [ ] Confirm stop placement before entry

### V2.0 (CISD — Recommended):
- [ ] After sweep, wait for first bar to CLOSE ABOVE 10 AM high (longs) or BELOW 10 AM low (shorts)
- [ ] Enter at open of NEXT bar
- [ ] Stop: **3 points beyond the LOW of the CISD bar** (longs) or HIGH (shorts)
- [ ] **Cancel if no CISD by 10:30 AM**

---

## POSITION SIZING

```
Account Risk ($) = Account Balance × 0.01   (1% rule)
Stop Distance ($) = Stop Points × $20 (NQ) or $2 (MNQ)
Contracts = Account Risk / Stop Distance (round DOWN)
```

**Example — $50,000 account, 25-pt stop, MNQ:**
- Account Risk = $500
- Stop per contract = 25 × $2 = $50
- Contracts = $500 / $50 = **10 MNQ contracts**

---

## TRADE MANAGEMENT

- [ ] Set **Target 1 (T1)** = Entry ± 1× Risk (1R)
- [ ] Set **Target 2 (T2)** = Entry ± 2× Risk (2R)
- [ ] At T1 hit → Close **33% of position**, move stop to **breakeven + 1 tick**
- [ ] At T2 hit → Close **33% more**, trail remainder with 5-min swing structure
- [ ] **Close all by 12:00 PM ET** if not already out

---

## STOP IS HIT

- [ ] Accept the loss. Do not add to the position.
- [ ] Do not re-enter the same day (V1.0 rule: one trade per day)
- [ ] Log the trade: date, direction, sweep size, SMT yes/no, outcome

---

## INVALIDATION — DO NOT ENTER IF:

- High-impact news within 15 min of 10:00 AM (without SMT)
- Bias score < 2 out of 3
- No clean sweep (wick extension < 5 pts)
- 10 AM candle is an inside bar
- It is already 10:30 AM and no CISD has formed (V2.0)
- Already in a trade from today
